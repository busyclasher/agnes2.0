# Role: Pitch and Demo Lead

## Mission

Own the SafePoint pitch, demo script, and judging story.

## Core Pitch

> SafePoint brings safety comprehension to the worker’s hand at the exact moment of risk.

## Demo Story

1. Show construction sign in English.
2. Explain the comprehension gap.
3. Scan sign with SafePoint.
4. Show translated risk card.
5. Play audio guidance.
6. Show generated pictogram card.
7. Draft near-miss report.
8. Close with point-of-risk safety comprehension.

## Key Lines

> This is not back-office compliance reporting. It is worker-side safety comprehension.

> SafePoint complements official training and safety procedures. It does not replace them.

> Agnes AI powers the full multimodal workflow: vision, reasoning, translation, image generation, video briefing, and report drafting.

## Q&A Prep

Likely questions:

- What if AI gets it wrong?
- Is this replacing safety officers?
- How do you prevent worker surveillance?
- Why Agnes AI?
- How is this different from Google Translate?
- Who pays for it?

## Definition of Done

- [ ] 2-minute pitch ready.
- [ ] Demo script ready.
- [ ] Backup demo ready.
- [ ] Safety caveats ready.
- [ ] Agnes API value clearly explained.
